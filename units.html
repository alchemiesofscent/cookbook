Units

The weights and measures the recipes name, with the metric range every page calculates from. The pound and the ounce have English names; the other units keep their Greek ones.

UnitEnglishMetricAuthority
λίτρα litrapound322–329 gHultsch 1882
οὐγγία oungiaounce26.8–27.4 gHultsch 1882 (one twelfth of the pound)
δραχμή drachme—3.35–3.43 gHultsch 1882 (one ninety-sixth of the pound)
ὁλκή holke—3.35–3.43 gequal to the drachma (Sprengel 1829; Berendes 1902)
μνᾶ mna—437 gthe Attic mina (conventional value)
ξέστης xestes—547 mlconventional value
κοτύλη kotyle—274 mlconventional value
χοῦς chous—3,282 mlconventional value
χοῖνιξ choinix—1,010–1,094 mlconventional values, 1.01–1.09 l

Where a page needs a weight for a volume (the scaler, the family comparisons), it counts the volume as olive oil at 0.91–0.92 g per ml.
