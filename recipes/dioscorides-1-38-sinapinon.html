σινάπινον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.38

Mustard oil

draft

Mustard oil is made by grinding mustard and soaking it in hot water, then mixing in oil and pressing the whole out together. The text gives no amounts or times.

Greek · Wellmann

τὸ δὲ σινάπινον σκευάζεται λεαινομένου τοῦ σινήπεως καὶ βρεχομένου ὕδατι θερμῷ, ἔπειτα μειγνυμένου ἐλαίου καὶ συνεκθλιβομένου. ποιεῖ δὲ πρὸς τὰ χρόνια ἀλγήματα μετασυγκριτικὸν ὄν.

English · approved translation

Mustard oil is prepared by grinding the mustard and soaking it in hot water, then mixing in oil and pressing the whole mixture out together. It is effective against chronic pains because it has an alterative action.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.38, ed. Wellmann.
