Recipe

ἐλατῖνον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.44

Palm-spathe oil

draft

Crushed palm spathe is steeped for three days in an equal weight of unripe-olive oil. It is then pressed out in a basket and the oil stored clean.

TimeSteep three daysNo temperatures are given.

Ingredients

Amount · metric

Palm spathe

Crushed and torn apart.

no amount given

Unripe-olive oil

ὀμφάκινον ἔλαιον · ἔλαιον ὀμφάκινον

Equal in weight to the spathe.

as printed

ἑκάτερα ἰσόσταθμα

Method

  1. 1

    Prepare the spathe. Crush and tear apart a date-palm spathe.

  2. 2

    Steep. Put it in a tub and pour unripe-olive oil over it, equal to it in weight. Leave it three days.

    Equal weight

    The text gives no amounts, only that spathe and oil weigh the same.

  3. 3

    Press. Move it to a basket and press it out.

  4. 4

    Store. Store the oil cleanly in a vessel, and use it.

Uses

Its capacity is like that of rose oil, but it does not soften the bowels.

The text

Greek · Wellmann

ἐλατίνου σκευασία· ἐλάτην θλάσας καὶ διακνίσας ἐμβαλών τε εἰς λουτῆρα ἐπίχει ὀμφάκινον ἔλαιον ἐπὶ ἡμέρας τρεῖς ἀνελόμενός τε εἰς σφυρίδα ἐξίπου· ἔστω δὲ ἑκάτερα ἰσόσταθμα· εἶτα ἀποθέμενος καθαρίως ἐν ἀγγείῳ χρῶ. δύναμιν δὲ ἔχει ἀναλογοῦσαν τῷ ῥοδίνῳ, κοιλίαν δὲ οὐ μαλάσσει.

English · approved translation

Crush and tear apart a date-palm spathe, put it into a tub, and pour oil of unripe olives over it. After three days, transfer it to a basket and press it out. The two ingredients should be equal in weight. Then store the oil cleanly in a vessel and use it. Its capacity corresponds to that of rose oil, but it does not soften the bowels.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.44, ed. Wellmann.
