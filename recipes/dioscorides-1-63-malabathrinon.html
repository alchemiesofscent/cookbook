μαλαβάθρινον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.63

Malabathron unguent

draft

Malabathron unguent has the same astringent ingredients as nard unguent, but more myrrh. The text gives nothing more: no other ingredients, amounts or method.

Ingredients

Amount · metric

Myrrh

σμύρνης · σμύρνα

More than in nard unguent.

as printed

πλεῖον

Method

  1. 1

    As for nard unguent. Use the same astringent ingredients as for nard unguent.

    The astringents

    In nard unguent (1.62) the oil is made astringent with camel hay.

  2. 2

    More myrrh. Add more myrrh than in nard unguent.

    How much more

    The text does not say; nard unguent itself gives no amount.

Uses

Warming; its capacity corresponds to that of saffron unguent and marjoram unguent.

The text

The approved English of the passage; turn on Greek (top right) to read Wellmann's Greek beside it.

Greek · Wellmann

τὸ δὲ μαλαβάθρινον ἔχει μὲν στύμματα ἃ καὶ ἡ νάρδος, σμύρνης δὲ πλεῖον· διὸ καὶ γίνεται θερμαντικόν, ἀναλογοῦν τῷ κροκίνῳ καὶ ἀμαρακίνῳ τὴν δύναμιν.

English · approved translation

Malabathron unguent contains the same astringent ingredients as nard unguent, but more myrrh. Consequently, it is warming, and its capacity corresponds to that of saffron unguent and marjoram unguent.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.63, ed. Wellmann.

Editor's notes

The oribasius-app Greek of 1.63 holds only this entry: the jasmine oil (ἰάσμη) that perfume-tables keeps under 1.63 comes from Wellmann's apparatus and is ported separately as dioscorides-1-63-iasmelaion. Nothing is cut.

The astringent ingredients (στύμματα) are those of nard unguent (1.62: camel hay); they are not listed here.
