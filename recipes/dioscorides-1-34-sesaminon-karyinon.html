σησάμινον καὶ καρύϊνον sesaminon kai karyinon

Sesame and royal-nut oil

Sesame oil family2 ingredients

Sesame oil and royal-nut oil are pressed from their seeds the same way as almond oil (1.33). Dioscorides gives no quantities.

Ingredients

  • σησάμου sesamou

    for sesame oil

    no amount given

  • βασιλικῶν καρύων basilikon karyon

    for royal-nut oil

    no amount given

Method

  1. Sesame oil Make sesame oil from sesame as for almond oil (1.33).

  2. Royal-nut oil Make royal-nut oil from royal nuts the same way.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.34 (Wellmann 38.10), ed. M. Wellmann, Berlin 1907, vol. 1, p. 38.

Translated by Alchemies of Scent (Sean Coughlin), 2026, from Wellmann’s text.
