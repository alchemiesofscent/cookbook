σησάμινον καὶ καρύϊνον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.34 (Wellmann 38.10)

Sesame and royal-nut oil

draft · Sesame oil

Two oils in one sentence: sesame oil, made from sesame, and royal-nut oil, made from royal nuts. Both are prepared in the same manner as the oils described above; the text gives no method or amounts of its own.

Ingredients

Amount · metric

Sesame for sesame oil

σησάμου · σήσαμον

no amount given

Royal nuts for royal-nut oil

βασιλικῶν καρύων · κάρυον

no amount given

Method

  1. 1

    Sesame oil. Make sesame oil from sesame in the same manner as the oils described above.

  2. 2

    Royal-nut oil. Make royal-nut oil from royal nuts in the same way.

Uses

The same capacity as balanos-nut oil (1.34).

The text

The approved English of the passage; turn on Greek (top right) to read Wellmann's Greek beside it.

Greek · Wellmann

ὁμοίως δὲ σκευάζεται τοῖς προειρημένοις τό τε σησάμινον ἐκ τοῦ σησάμου καὶ τὸ καρύινον ἐκ τῶν βασιλικῶν καρύων συντιθέμενον. δύναμιν δὲ ἔχουσι τὴν αὐτὴν τῷ βαλανίνῳ.

English · approved translation

Sesame oil is similarly prepared from sesame, in the same manner as the oils described above, as is royal-nut oil, compounded from royal nuts. They have the same capacity as balanos-nut oil.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.34 (Wellmann 38.10), ed. Wellmann.

Editor's notes

This record is in two families, sesaminon and karyinon (families.json lists it under both); it names the first, sesaminon.

Chapter 1.34 holds two entries; this is the second, from ὁμοίως δὲ σκευάζεται τοῖς προειρημένοις to the end of the chapter. The balanos oil before it is ported as dioscorides-1-34-balaninon. Wellmann prints no section number inside the chapter; the locator gives perfume-tables' Wellmann page and line.

One entry covers two oils, sesame oil and royal-nut oil, made like the oils described before. Both have the same capacity as balanos oil.
