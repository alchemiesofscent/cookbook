μενδήσιον mendesion

Mendesian unguent

Mendesian unguent family5 ingredients

Mendesian unguent is made from balanos oil, myrrh, cassia and resin. Dioscorides says it acts like metopion, only more weakly. Dioscorides gives no quantities.

Ingredients

  • βαλανίνου ἐλαίου balaninou elaiou

    no amount given

  • σμύρνης smyrnes

    no amount given

  • κασσίας kassias

    no amount given

  • ῥητίνης rhetines

    no amount given

  • κινάμωμον kinamomon

    alternative

    A little, added by some makers after the weighing.

    no amount given

Method

  1. Combine the ingredients Combine balanos oil, myrrh, cassia and resin.

    Together Dioscorides says an ingredient not boiled with the rest does not give up its strength.

Other ways

Cinnamon after the weighing Some makers add a little cinnamon after the weighing; Dioscorides counts it wasted, since what is not boiled with the rest gives up none of its strength.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.59.3, ed. M. Wellmann, Berlin 1907, vol. 1, p. 55.
