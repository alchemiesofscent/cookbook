κῦφι kyphi

Kyphi

10 ingredientsTime · steep

g of σταφίδος (raisins) as written

Kyphi is an incense, not an oil. Raisins are ground with aged wine and myrrh, the other aromatics are pounded and worked in, and the mass is bound with honey boiled thick and melted resin. Dioscorides says the priests in Egypt use it in abundance.

Time

Steep
one day, everything together

The honey is boiled until it is glue-like; no other times and no temperatures are given.

Method

  1. Make the raisin paste Remove the seeds from 12 mnai (5.2 kg) of plump raisins, chop them, and grind them smooth with 9 xestai (4.9 l) of aged wine and 12 drachmai (40–41 g) of myrrh.

  2. Add the other ingredients Pound and sift the other ingredients and mix them into the raisin paste.

    The other ingredients ½ xestes each of cyperus and juniper berries, and 1 mna each of aromatic reed, aspalathos and rush; the resin and honey go in later.

  3. Steep Leave everything to steep together for one day.

  4. Boil the honey with the resin Boil 2 mnai (873 g) of honey until it is glue-like, then carefully mix in 5 mnai (2.2 kg) of purified resin, melted.

  5. Combine and store Rub the rest of the mixture thoroughly into the honey and resin, and store it in an earthenware vessel.
