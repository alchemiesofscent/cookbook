μηλῖνον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.45.1

Quince oil

draft · Quince oil

ml of olive oil the written batch

Olive oil and water are left for a day with crushed palm spathe and schoinos, then boiled, and the oil is strained off. Quinces are set on a mat over the mouth of the vessel, covered with cloths, and left until the oil has drawn the capacity from the fruit.

Method

  1. 1

    Prepare the oil. Mix 6 sextarii of olive oil with 10 sextarii of water. Add 3 ounces of crushed palm spathe and 1 ounce of schoinos, and leave it for one day.

  2. 2

    Boil and strain. Boil it. Then strain off the oil into a broad-mouthed vessel.

    The water

    The text does not say what becomes of the water; only the oil is strained off.

  3. 3

    Set the quinces over the oil. Lay a reed screen or a loosely woven rush mat over the mouth, and set quinces on it.

  4. 4

    Cover and wait. Cover them with cloths and leave them as many days as it takes the oil to draw the capacity from the fruit.

    How many days

    The text says only “a sufficient number of days”.

Greek · Wellmann

μηλίνον σκευασία· ἐλαίου ξέστας ἕξ, ὕδατος ξέστας δέκα μείξας προσέμβαλε σπάθης τεθλασμένης οὐγγίας τρεῖς, σχοίνου οὐγγίαν μίαν καὶ ἐάσας ἡμέραν μίαν ἕψησον, εἶτα ἀπηθήσας τὸ ἔλαιον βάλε εἰς ἀγγεῖον πλατύστομον, ἐπιθείς τε ῥῖπον ἐκ καλάμου ἢ ψίαθον ἀραιὰν ἄνωθέν τε ἐπιθεὶς κυδώνια μῆλα καὶ περικαλύψας ἱματίοις ἔασον ἡμέρας ἱκανάς, ἕως ἂν ἐπισπάσηται τὴν ἀπὸ τῶν μήλων δύναμιν τὸ ἔλαιον.

English · approved translation

Mix six xestai of olive oil with ten xestai of water. Add three ounces of crushed palm spathe and one ounce of aromatic rush, leave the mixture for one day, and then boil it. Next, filter the oil and transfer it to a broad-mouthed container. Lay a reed screen or a loosely woven rush mat over the mouth, place quinces on top, cover them with cloths, and leave them there for a sufficient number of days, until the oil draws the capacity from the fruit.
