Must unguent

draft · Must unguent

The simple sweet-must unguent. Unripe-olive oil, aromatics and grape must are put in a vessel packed round with grape marc, stirred twice a day for thirty days, then pressed out and stored. No amounts are given.

Method

  1. 1

    Fill the vessel. Put unripe-olive oil, schoinos, calamus, Celtic nard, palm spathe, aspalathos, melilot, costus and grape must together in a vessel.

    No amounts

    The text names what goes in but gives no amounts and no preparation of the aromatics.

  2. 2

    Pack with grape marc. Pack grape marc around the vessel.

    Around, not in

    The marc is packed round the vessel that holds the aromatics, wine and oil; it does not go into the unguent.

  3. 3

    Stir for thirty days. Stir twice a day for thirty days.

  4. 4

    Press and store. Press it out and store it.

Uses

Warming, softening and relaxing: for chills, every disorder of the sinews, and the womb; as an emollient, it relieves more than any other fatigue remedy.

Greek · Wellmann

γλεύκινον δὲ σκευάζεται τὸ ἁπλοῦν ἔκ τε ἐλαίου ὀμφακίνου καὶ σχοίνου καὶ καλάμου καὶ νάρδου Κελτικῆς καὶ σπάθης καὶ ἀσπαλάθου καὶ μελιλώτου καὶ κόστου καὶ γλεύκους, συμπεριτιθεμένων τῶν στεμφύλων τῷ ἔχοντι ἀγγείῳ τὰ ἀρώματα καὶ τὸν οἶνον καὶ τὸ ἔλαιον. κινεῖται δὲ ἐπὶ ἡμέρας τριάκοντα δὶς τῆς ἡμέρας, καὶ μετὰ ταῦτα ἐκπιεσθὲν ἀποτίθεται. δύναμιν δὲ ἔχει θερμαντικήν, μαλακτικήν, ἀνετικήν, ποιοῦσαν πρὸς τὰ ῥίγη καὶ τὰ νευρικὰ πάντα καὶ τὰ ἐν ὑστέρᾳ. παντὸς δὲ ἀκόπου μᾶλλον ὠφελεῖ μαλακτικὸν ὄν.

English · approved translation

The simple sweet-must unguent is prepared from oil of unripe olives, camel hay, sweet flag, Celtic nard, palm spathe, aspalathos, melilot, costus, and sweet must. Grape pomace is packed around the vessel containing the aromatics, wine, and oil. The mixture is stirred twice daily for thirty days; it is then pressed out and stored. Its capacity is warming, softening, and relaxing, and it is effective against chills, every disorder of the sinews, and disorders of the uterus. Because it is emollient, it gives more relief than any other fatigue-relieving application.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.57, ed. Wellmann.

Editor's notes

The simple form (τὸ ἁπλοῦν); no amounts are given.

The Greek and English then call the must "wine" (τὸν οἶνον) in the vessel; it is not a separate ingredient.

The grape marc is moved to the working materials: it is packed around the vessel, not put into it.
