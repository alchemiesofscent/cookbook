γλεύκινον gleukinon

Must unguent

Must unguent family9 ingredientsTime · stir

Simple must unguent is made from unripe-olive oil, grape must and aromatics (schoinos, calamus, Celtic nard, palm spathe, aspalathos, melilot and costus) in a vessel packed round with grape marc. The mixture is stirred twice a day for thirty days, then pressed out and stored. Dioscorides gives no quantities.

Method

  1. Fill the vessel Put unripe-olive oil, schoinos, calamus, Celtic nard, palm spathe, aspalathos, melilot, costus and grape must together in a vessel.

  2. Pack with grape marc Pack grape marc around the vessel.

    Around, not in The marc is packed round the vessel that holds the aromatics, must and oil; it does not go into the unguent.

  3. Stir for thirty days Stir twice a day for thirty days.

  4. Press and store Press it out.

    Store it.
