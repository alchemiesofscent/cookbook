σαμψουχῖνον sampsouchinon

Marjoram oil

7 ingredientsTime · steep, steep again

Marjoram oil is unripe-olive oil steeped with wild thyme, cassia, southernwood, water-mint flowers, myrtle leaves and marjoram, pounded together. The herbs steep for four days and are pressed out, then the same amount of fresh herbs is steeped for the same time and pressed out again. Each herb is taken by estimate, with an eye to its strength, and only enough oil is used not to overpower them.

Method

  1. Pound the herbs Pound the wild thyme, cassia, southernwood, water-mint flowers, myrtle leaves and marjoram together in one vessel.

    How much of each Take each herb by estimate, with an eye to its strength.

  2. Add the oil Pour on oil of unripe olives, only so much that it does not overpower the strength of the herbs steeped in it.

  3. Steep and press Leave it for four days, then press it out.

  4. A second steeping Steep the same amount of the same herbs, fresh, for the same time, and press them out.

    In which oil Use the oil from the first pressing; Dioscorides says the second steeping makes it more potent.

What to aim for

  • Dioscorides advises choosing oil that is dark olive in colour, smells strongly of marjoram and is moderately pungent.
  • According to Dioscorides, a second steeping with fresh herbs makes it more potent.
