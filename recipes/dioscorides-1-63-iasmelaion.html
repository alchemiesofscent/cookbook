Recipe

ἰασμέλαιον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.63.apparatus

Iasmelaion

draft translation

ml of sesame oil the written batch

A Persian oil called iasme: two ounces of the white flowers of the violet are steeped in an Italian xestes of sesame oil and replaced with fresh ones, as for lily oil.

Method

  1. 1

    Steep the flowers. Put two ounces of the white flowers of the violet into an Italian xestes of sesame oil.

  2. 2

    Change the flowers. Replace the flowers with fresh ones in turn, as described for lily oil.

    As for lily oil

    The Greek refers back to lily oil (κρίνινον) for the method and does not repeat it; it gives no times.

What to aim for

  • Its scent is heavy, so that many do not find it pleasant.

Uses

Used by the Persians at banquets for its fragrance; in the baths, over the whole body, for bodies that need warming and relaxing.

Greek · Wellmann

ἡ δὲ καλουμένη ἰάσμη παρὰ Πέρσαις σκευάζεται ἐκ τῶν ἀνθῶν τῶν λευκῶν τοῦ ἰοῦ, τούτων οὐγγίων βʹ ἐμβαλλομένων Ἰταλικῷ ξέστῃ σησαμίνου ἐλαίου καὶ ἀμειβομένων ἤγουν ἀλλασσομένων ὡς ἐπὶ τοῦ κρινίνου εἴρηται. ἡ δὲ τούτου χρῆσις παρὰ τὰς ἑστιάσεις εὐωδίας ἕνεκα παρὰ Πέρσαις παραλαμβάνεται. ἁρμόζει δὲ καὶ ὅλῳ τῷ σώματι κατὰ τὰ λουτρὰ ἐπὶ τῶι θερμασίας καὶ χαλάσεως δεομένων σωμάτων. βαρυτέραν δὲ ἔχει τὴν εὐωδίαν, ὡς πολλοὺς αὐτῆς μηδὲ (μή p) ἡδέως ἀντιλαμβάνεσθαι

English · draft translation from the Greek, not yet approved

The so-called iasme is prepared among the Persians from the white flowers of the violet: two ounces of these are put into an Italian xestes of sesame oil and exchanged, that is, replaced, as has been said for lily oil. Among the Persians it is used at banquets for the sake of its fragrance. It is also suitable for the whole body in the baths, for bodies that need warming and relaxing. Its fragrance is rather heavy, so that many do not find it pleasant.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.63.apparatus, ed. Wellmann.
