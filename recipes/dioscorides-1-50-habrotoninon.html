ἁβροτόνινον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.50

Southernwood oil

draft

g of aromatised oil the written batch

Southernwood leaves are steeped for a night and a day in oil already aromatised as for henna oil, then the oil is pressed out. For an oil of several steepings, the first leaves are taken out and others steeped and pressed in their place.

TimeSteep a night and a dayFurther steepings have no time of their own. No temperatures are given.

Method

  1. 1

    Steep the leaves. Put 8 lb of southernwood leaves into 9 lb 5 oz of the aromatised oil and leave them for a night and a day.

    The oil

    Oil aromatised as for henna oil (kyprinon), described in 1.55.

  2. 2

    Press. Press out the oil.

  3. 3

    Further steepings. For an oil of several steepings, take out the first leaves, put in other leaves, steep them in the oil, and press it out.

    How many leaves

    The text gives no amount for the further leaves.

Uses

Warming: for closures and indurations in the uterus; it brings down the menses and afterbirth.

The text

Greek · Wellmann

ἁβροτονίνου σκευασία· ἐκ τοῦ εἰς τὸ κύπρινον ἠρωματισμένου ἐλαίου λίτρας ἐννέα οὐγγίας πέντε ἁβροτόνου φύλλων λίτρας ὀκτὼ ἔμβαλε καὶ ἀποβρέξας νύκτα καὶ ἡμέραν ἐξίπου. πολύβροχον δʼ εἰ βούλει ποιῆσαι, ἐκβαλὼν τὰ πρῶτα φύλλα, ἐμβαλὼν δὲ ἕτερα φύλλα ἐναποβρέχων ἐκπίεζε. δύναμιν δὲ ἔχει θερμαντικήν, ποιοῦσαν πρὸς τὰς ἐν ὑστέρᾳ μύσεις καὶ σκληρίας, ἄγον ἔμμηνα καὶ δεύτερα.

English · approved translation

Into nine litrai and five ounces of the oil aromatized for making henna oil, put eight litrai of southernwood leaves. Steep them for one night and one day, then press out the oil. If you wish to make it with repeated infusions, remove the first leaves, add other leaves, steep them in the oil, and press it out. It has a warming capacity, effective for closures and indurations in the uterus, and it brings down the menses and afterbirth.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.50, ed. Wellmann.
