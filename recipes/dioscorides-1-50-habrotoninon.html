ἁβροτόνινον habrotoninon

Southernwood oil

2 ingredientsTime · steep

g of ἐλαίου (Aromatised oil) as written

Southernwood oil is made from southernwood leaves and oil already aromatised as for henna oil (1.55). The leaves are steeped in the oil for a night and a day and pressed out. For an oil of several steepings, the first leaves are taken out and others are steeped and pressed in their place.

Time

Steep
a night and a day

Further steepings have no time of their own. No temperatures are given.

Ingredients

  • ἐλαίου elaiou

    Oil already aromatised as for henna oil (kyprinon, 1.55).

    9 pounds 5 ounces

    3–3.1 kg

    λίτρας ἐννέα οὐγγίας πέντε

    54% of the batch

  • ἁβροτόνου φύλλων habrotonou phyllon

    The leaves; other leaves again for each further steeping.

    8 pounds

    2.6 kg

    λίτρας ὀκτὼ

    46% of the batch

Metric figures are ranges, from the pound (λίτρα) of 322–329 g and the other units; the scaler changes them and leaves the ancient amounts as written.

Method

  1. Steep the leaves Steep 8 lb (2.6 kg) of southernwood leaves in 9 lb 5 oz (3–3.1 kg) of the aromatised oil for a night and a day.

    The oil Oil aromatised as for henna oil (kyprinon, 1.55).

  2. Press Press out the oil.

  3. Further steepings Replace the first leaves with other leaves, for an oil of several steepings.

    Steep them in the oil and press it out again.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.50, ed. M. Wellmann, Berlin 1907, vol. 1, pp. 46–47.
