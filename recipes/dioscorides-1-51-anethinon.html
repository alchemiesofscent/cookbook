ἀνηθίνον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.51

Dill oil

draft · Dill oil

g of olive oil the written batch

Dill flowers are steeped in olive oil for one day, then pressed out by hand and the oil stored. For a double-steeped oil, fresh dill flowers are steeped the same way.

TimeSteep one dayA double steeping is done "the same way". No temperatures are given.

Method

  1. 1

    Steep the flowers. Steep 11 lb 8 oz of dill flowers in 8 lb 9 oz of olive oil for one day.

  2. 2

    Press and store. Press them out with your hands and store the oil.

  3. 3

    A double steeping. For a double-steeped oil, steep fresh dill flowers the same way.

    In which oil

    Not stated; "double-steeped" (δίβροχον) suggests the oil already made.

Uses

Softens and opens the parts around the uterus; for recurrent chills; it warms the body, relieves fatigue and helps pains in the joints.

Greek · Wellmann

ἀνηθίνου σκευασία· ἐλαίου λίτρας ὀκτὼ οὐγγίας ἐννέα, ἀνήθου ἄνθους λίτρας ἕνδεκα οὐγγίας ὀκτὼ ἐναπόβρεχε τῷ ἐλαίῳ ἡμέραν μίαν, εἶτα ἐκπίεζε ταῖς χερσὶ καὶ ἀποτίθεσο. ἐὰν δὲ θέλῃς δίβροχον ποιῆσαι, πρόσφατον ἀνήθου ἄνθος ὡσαύτως ἀπόβρεχε. δύναται δὲ μαλάσσειν τὰ περὶ μήτραν καὶ ἀναστομοῦν, ἁρμόζον πρὸς τὰ περιοδικὰ ῥίγη, θερμαῖνον καὶ κόπους λύον καὶ ἀλγήματα ἄρθρων ὠφελοῦν.

English · approved translation

Steep eleven litrai and eight ounces of dill flowers for one day in eight litrai and nine ounces of olive oil. Then press them out with your hands and store the oil. If you wish to make a double-infusion oil, steep fresh dill flowers in the same way. It can soften and open the parts around the uterus. It is suitable for recurrent chills, warms the body and relieves fatigue, and benefits pains in the joints.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.51, ed. Wellmann.
