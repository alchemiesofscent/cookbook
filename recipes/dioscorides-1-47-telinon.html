Fenugreek oil

draft · Fenugreek oil

g of olive oil the written batch

Fenugreek, calamus and cyperus are steeped in olive oil for seven days, stirred three times a day, then pressed out. Some use cardamom and balsam wood instead; others first make the oil astringent and then steep the fenugreek in it.

Method

  1. 1

    Steep. Put 9 pounds of fenugreek, 1 pound of calamus and 2 pounds of cyperus into 5 pounds of olive oil. Leave them seven days, stirring three times each day.

    Calamus

    The English calls it aromatic reed (κάλαμος).

  2. 2

    Press and store. Press out the oil and store it.

Other ways

Other aromatics. Some steep cardamom with it instead of the calamus, and balsam wood instead of the cyperus.

Oil prepared first. Others first make the oil astringent with these, then steep the fenugreek in it and press it out.

“These”: the text does not say whether the calamus and cyperus or the cardamom and balsam wood are meant.

What to aim for

  • Choose oil that is fresh, does not smell too strongly of fenugreek, cleanses the hands, and tastes bittersweet: this kind is best.

Uses

Softens abscesses and brings them to coction: for indurations of the uterus; by instillation in difficult labour when the parts are dry; for inflammations of the anus; as an enema for tenesmus; scours away dandruff, scurf and freckles; with wax, for burns and chilblains; in preparations for the complexion.

Greek · Wellmann

τηλίνου σκευασία· τήλεως λίτρας ἐννέα, ἐλαίου λίτρας πέντε, καλάμου λίτραν μίαν, κυπέρου λίτρας δύο ἀπόβρεχε τῷ ἐλαίῳ ἐπὶ ἡμέρας ἑπτά, κινῶν ἑκάστης ἡμέρας τρίς, εἶτα ἐξίπου καὶ ἀποτίθεσο. οἱ δὲ ἀντὶ μὲν τοῦ καλάμου καρδάμωμον, ἀντὶ δὲ τῆς κυπέρου ξυλοβάλσαμον συναποβρέχουσιν. οἱ δὲ προστύφουσι τὸ ἔλαιον τούτοις καὶ μετὰ ταῦτα τὴν τῆλιν ἀποβρέχοντες ἐξιποῦσιν. δύναμιν δὲ ἔχει μαλακτικὴν ἀποστημάτων, πεπτικήν. μάλιστα 2 δὲ ἁρμόζει τοῖς περὶ μήτραν πᾶσι σκιρρώμασι, δυστοκούσαις τε ἔγχυμα, ἐπειδὰν κατάξηρα τὰ περὶ τοὺς τόπους γένηται, προεκκριθέντων τῶν ὑγρῶν, πρός τε τὰς ἐν δακτυλίῳ φλεγμονάς, καὶ ἐγκλύζεται ἐπὶ τῶν τεινεσμῶν ὠφελίμως, πίτυρά τε καὶ ἀχῶρας σμήχει καὶ κατακαύματα καὶ χίμετλα ὠφελεῖ μετὰ κηροῦ, σμήχει τε ἐφηλίδας καὶ εἰς στιλβώματα μείγνυται. ἐκλέγου δὲ τὸ πρόσφατον καὶ μὴ λίαν παρεμφαῖνον τὴν τῆς τήλεως ὀσμήν, σμῆχον τὰς χεῖρας, γλυκύπικρον ἐν τῇ γεύσει· τὸ γὰρ τοιοῦτον κάλλιστον.

English · approved translation

Take nine litrai of fenugreek, five litrai of olive oil, one litra of aromatic reed, and two litrai of cyperus. Steep them in the oil for seven days, stirring three times each day; then press out the oil and store it. Some steep cardamom with it instead of the aromatic reed and balsam wood instead of the cyperus. Others first render the oil astringent with these ingredients, then steep the fenugreek in it and press it out.

It has the capacity to soften abscesses and bring them to coction. It is especially suitable for every induration around the uterus. It is used as an instillation for women in difficult labor when the parts have become completely dry because the fluids were discharged beforehand, and also for inflammations of the anus. It is beneficially administered as an enema for tenesmus. It scours away dandruff and scurf; combined with wax, it benefits burns and chilblains. It also scours away freckles and is mixed into preparations for beautifying the complexion. Choose oil that is fresh, does not display the smell of fenugreek too strongly, cleanses the hands, and has a bittersweet taste, for this kind is best.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.47, ed. Wellmann.
