μηλῖνον melinon

Quince oil

Quince oil family5 ingredientsTime · rest, boil, under the quinces

ml of ἐλαίου (oil) as written

Quince oil is olive oil scented with quinces. The oil is left for a day with water, crushed palm spathe and schoinos, boiled and strained, then set under quinces laid on a mat over the mouth of the vessel and covered with cloths. The quinces sit above the oil, not in it, until it has drawn their capacity.

Method

  1. Prepare the oil Mix 6 xestai (3.3 l) of olive oil with 10 xestai (5.5 l) of water. Add 3 ounces (80–82 g) of crushed palm spathe and 1 ounce (27 g) of schoinos, and leave it for one day.

  2. Boil and strain Boil the mixture, then strain the oil off into a broad-mouthed vessel.

    The water Only the oil goes into the vessel; the water and the solids are left behind.

  3. Set the quinces over the oil Lay a reed screen or a loosely woven rush mat over the mouth, and set quinces on it.

  4. Cover and wait Cover them with cloths and leave them as many days as it takes the oil to draw the capacity from the fruit.

    How many days No number is fixed; leave them until the oil has drawn the capacity from the fruit.
