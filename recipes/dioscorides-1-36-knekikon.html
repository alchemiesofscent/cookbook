κνήκινον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.36 (Wellmann 39.5)

Safflower oil

draft

A report, not a recipe: safflower oil is prepared in the same manner as the oils before it. The text gives no ingredients, amounts or method of its own.

Ingredients

The text names no ingredients.

Method

A report, not a recipe: the text names or describes the preparation without giving a method.

Uses

The same as Cnidian-berry oil, but milder.

The text

The approved English of the passage; turn on Greek (top right) to read Wellmann's Greek beside it.

Greek · Wellmann

ὡσαύτως δὲ καὶ κνήκινον σκευάζεται. δύναμις δὲ ἡ αὐτὴ τῷ διὰ τοῦ Κνιδίου κόκκου, ἀνειμένη δὲ μᾶλλον.

English · approved translation

Safflower oil is prepared in the same manner. Its capacity is the same as that of Cnidian-berry oil, but rather milder.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.36 (Wellmann 39.5), ed. Wellmann.

Editor's notes

A report, not a recipe: safflower oil is said to be prepared in the same manner as Cnidian-berry oil, with the same capacity but milder. The text names no ingredient (the safflower, κνῆκος, only in the name of the oil).

Chapter 1.36 holds two entries; this is the second, from ὡσαύτως δὲ καὶ κνήκινον to the end of the chapter. The Cnidian-berry oil before it is ported as dioscorides-1-36-knidion. The locator gives perfume-tables' Wellmann page and line.
