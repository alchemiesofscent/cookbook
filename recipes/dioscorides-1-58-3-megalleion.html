μεγάλλειον megalleion

Megalleion

2 ingredientsTime · boil the resin

Megalleion is marjoram unguent made with more resin, the terebinth boiled down first. Dioscorides records it as no longer in use in his day. According to Dioscorides, resin goes into unguents for colour and thickness, not to preserve or sweeten them.

Time

Boil the resin
until it is odourless

Until the resin has lost its smell; no fixed time.

Ingredients

  • ῥητίνη rhetine

    for colour and thickness

    More than in marjoram unguent.

    no amount given

  • τερεβινθίνη terebinthine

    Boiled down until it has no smell.

    no amount given

Method

  1. Boil the resin Boil the terebinth resin down until it has no smell.

    How to boil it The boiling of resin is set out under resin (1.71).

  2. Make it as marjoram unguent Prepare the unguent as marjoram unguent, but with more resin.

    Marjoram unguent See marjoram unguent (1.58).

What to aim for

  • Dioscorides says resin goes into unguents for colour and thickness, not to preserve them or to make them pleasant.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.58.3, ed. M. Wellmann, Berlin 1907, vol. 1, p. 54.
