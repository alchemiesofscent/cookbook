Plain lily oil

draft · Lily oil

The simple form of lily oil, made from balanos oil, or another oil, and lilies. Dioscorides gives no amounts and no method; the oil made in Phoenicia and Egypt is considered superior.

Method

What to aim for

Uses

Warming, softening and opening: for occlusions and inflammations of the uterus, and among the most useful remedies for women's complaints; for scaly sores, pimples, dandruff and eruptions; it quickly clears bruises to the colour of the skin around; drunk, it carries bile down through the bowels and promotes urination. It is bad for the stomach and causes nausea.

Greek · Wellmann

ἔνιοι δὲ τὸ ἁπλοῦν ἐξ ἐλαίου βαλανίνου ἢ ἄλλου καὶ 5 κρίνου σκευάζουσι. δοκεῖ δὲ διαφέρειν τὸ ἐν Φοινίκῃ καὶ ἐν Αἰγύπτῳ γινόμενον· ἄριστον δέ ἐστιν αὐτοῦ τὸ ὄζον κρίνων. δύναμιν δὲ ἔχει θερμαντικήν, μαλακτικήν, ἀναστομωτικὴν τῶν περὶ μήτραν μύσεων καὶ φλεγμονῶν, καὶ καθόλου πάντων ἐστὶ χρησιμώτατον πρὸς τὰ γυναικεῖα. ἁρμόζει δὲ καὶ πρὸς ἀχῶρας, ἰόνθους, πίτυρα, ἐκζέματα, μώλωπάς τε ταχέως μεταβάλλει καὶ ὁμόχρους ποιεῖ, καὶ καθόλου ἐστὶν ἀραιωτικόν. ἄγει δὲ καὶ χολὴν κατὰ κοιλίαν πινόμενον, κινεῖ δὲ καὶ οὔρησιν. ἐστι δὲ κακοστόμαχον καὶ ναυτίας ποιητικόν.

English · approved translation

5. Some prepare the simple form from balanos oil or another oil and lily. The product made in Phoenicia and Egypt is considered superior; the finest is the one that smells of lilies. It has a warming, softening, and opening capacity for occlusions and inflammations in the region of the uterus and, in general, is among the most useful remedies for women’s complaints. It is also suitable for scaly sores, pimples, dandruff, and skin eruptions. It quickly disperses bruises and restores them to the color of the surrounding skin; in general, it has a rarefying action. When drunk, it also carries bile down through the bowels and promotes urination. It is harmful to the stomach and causes nausea.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.52.5, ed. Wellmann.
