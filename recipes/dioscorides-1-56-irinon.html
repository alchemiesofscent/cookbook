ἴρινον irinon

Iris unguent

Iris unguent family3 ingredientsTime · boil

g of ἐλαίου (oil) as written

Iris unguent is made by steeping iris in oil first made astringent and fragrant with palm spathe. Finely chopped spathe is boiled with the oil and water in a bronze vessel until the oil takes on the scent, and the oil is strained into a honey-coated bowl. Dioscorides ranks highest the unguent that smells of iris alone, such as is made at Perge in Pamphylia and Elis in Achaea.

Method

  1. Prepare the oil Chop 6 lb 8 oz (2.1–2.2 kg) of palm spathe as finely as possible.

    Mix it with 9 lb 5 oz (3–3.1 kg) of oil and 10 kotylai (2.7 l) of water in a bronze vessel.

    Boil until the oil takes on the scent.

  2. Strain Strain it into a mixing bowl coated with honey.

  3. Steep the iris Steep iris in this astringed oil to make the first iris unguent.

    Amounts and time As in the other method (1.56.2): the same weight of chopped iris as of oil, for two days and two nights.

What to aim for

  • Dioscorides ranks highest the unguent that smells of iris alone and of nothing else.
  • Dioscorides names the iris unguent of Perge in Pamphylia and of Elis in Achaea as of this kind.
