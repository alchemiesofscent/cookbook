Whitened oil

draft

ml of olive oil the written batch

Pale, young olive oil is whitened in the sun. It is set out in a wide earthenware vessel and beaten with a ladle each day so that it changes and foams; on the eighth day soaked fenugreek and split resinous pine go in for eight days more. The oil is then drawn off and stored over dried melilot and iris, or, if it is not yet white, sunned and worked again.

Method

  1. 1

    Set the oil in the sun. Pour 100 kotylai of oil, pale in colour and no more than a year old, into a new, wide-mouthed earthenware vessel, and set it in the sun.

  2. 2

    Beat it every day. Each day lift the oil with a ladle from the centre and pour it back down from high above, so that the constant stirring and beating make it change and foam.

    Each day

    The fenugreek goes in on the eighth day; the text does not say whether the beating goes on after that.

  3. 3

    Add fenugreek and pine. On the eighth day soak 50 drachmas of clean fenugreek in warm water. When it is soft, put it into the oil without draining off the water. Also add an equal weight of a firebrand of the most resinous pine, split into fine pieces.

    An equal weight

    The Greek says only “equal weights” (τὰς ἴσας ὁλκὰς); presumably the 50 drachmas of the fenugreek.

  4. 4

    Leave eight more days. Leave it as it is for another eight days.

  5. 5

    Draw off and store. Draw off the oil with the ladle. If it has come out as it should, rinse a new vessel with aged wine, lay 11 holkai of dried melilot garlands and as much iris in the bottom, pour in the oil and store it.

  6. 6

    If it is not yet white. If it has not succeeded, set it in the sun again and keep working it until it becomes white.

What to aim for

  • Start from oil that is pale in colour and no more than a year old.
  • The daily beating should make the oil change and foam.
  • The oil should end white; if it is not, sun it and work it again.

Greek · Wellmann

λευκαίνεται δὲ ἔλαιον οὕτως· λαβὼν τὸ τῇ χρόᾳ μὲν λευκὸν τὴν ἡλικίαν δὲ μὴ πλέον ἐνιαυσίου ἔγχεε εἰς κεραμεοῦν ἀγγεῖον πλατύστομον καινόν — ἔστωσαν δὲ μέτρῳ κοτύλαι ἑκατόν —, εἶτα θεὶς ὑφ᾿ ἥλιον ἀνάχει κόγχῳ καθ᾿ ἑκάστην ἡμέραν κατὰ τὸ μέσον, ὑψόθεν τῇ καταφορᾷ χρώμενος, ἵνα τῇ συνεχεῖ κυκήσει καὶ πληγῇ μεταβάλληται καὶ ἀφρίσῃ. τῇ δὲ ὀγδόῃ 4 ἡμέρᾳ βρέξας τήλεως καθαρᾶς δραχμὰς πεντήκοντα ἐν ὕδατι θερμῷ ἔμβαλε μαλακὴν γενομένην εἰς τὸ προειρημένον ἔλαιον χωρὶς τοῦ στραγγίσαι τὸ ὕδωρ· προσαπόδος δὲ καὶ δαλὸν πιτυίνης ὡς ὅτι λιπαρωτάτης καὶ εἰς λεπτὰ κατεσχισμένης τὰς ἴσας ὁλκὰς καὶ οὕτως ἔασον ἄλλας ὀκτὼ ἡμέρας διελθεῖν. μετὰ ταύτας δὲ ἀνάχει τῷ κόγχῳ τὸ ἔλαιον, τό τε λοιπόν, εἰ μὲν εἵη τοῦ τέλους τετευχός, εἰς καινὸν ἀγγεῖον προκεκλυσμένον οἴνῳ παλαιῷ κατεράσας ἀποτίθεσο, προυπεστρωμένων μελιλώτου στεφανίσκων αὔων ὁλκῶν ἕνδεκα καὶ ἴρεως τῆς ἴσης. εἰ δὲ μή, πάλιν ἐν ἡλίῳ θετέον αὐτὸ καὶ ἐργαστέον, ἄχρις οὗ λευκὸν γένηται.

English · approved translation

Oil is whitened as follows. Take oil that is pale in color and no more than a year old, and pour it into a new, wide-mouthed earthenware vessel; the measured quantity should be one hundred cotylai. Set it in the sun and each day lift the oil with a ladle from the center, pouring it back down from high above, so that the continual agitation and beating cause it to change and foam.

4. On the eighth day, soak fifty drachmas of clean fenugreek in warm water. When it has softened, put it into the oil described above without draining away the water. Also add an equal weight of a pine firebrand, taken from the most resinous pine possible and split into fine pieces. Leave it in this state for another eight days. When those days have passed, draw off the oil with the ladle. If it has reached the desired result, pour it into a new vessel that has previously been rinsed with aged wine, after first laying in the bottom eleven holkai of dried melilot garlands and an equal quantity of iris; then store it. If it has not succeeded, set it in the sun again and continue working it until it becomes white.

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.30.3-4, ed. Wellmann.

Editor's notes

The cut runs from λευκαίνεται δὲ ἔλαιον οὕτως, in the middle of section 3, to the end of section 4; the English likewise begins in its paragraph 3 at "Oil is whitened as follows."

Wellmann prints the section number 4 inside a sentence (τῇ δὲ ὀγδόῃ 4 ἡμέρᾳ); section 4 really begins at τῇ δὲ ὀγδόῃ.

Reader pass: the aged wine, used only to rinse the storage vessel, is moved to the working materials. The warm water stays an ingredient: it goes into the oil with the fenugreek.
