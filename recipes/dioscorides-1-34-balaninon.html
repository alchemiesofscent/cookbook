Recipe

βαλάνινον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.34 (Wellmann 38.5–10)

Balanos oil

draft · Balanos oil

Balanos-nut oil is made likewise, in the same way as the oils described just before it. The text gives no method, ingredients or amounts of its own.

Ingredients

The text names no ingredients.

Method

  1. 1

    Make the oil. Make it likewise, in the same way as the oils described just before.

    Which oils

    The text does not say; the oil described just before is almond oil (1.33).

Uses

Clears spots, freckles, facial eruptions and dark scars, and softens the bowels, but is harmful to the stomach; dropped into the ear with goose fat, for earache and noises and whistling in the ears.

The text

The approved English of the passage; turn on Greek (top right) to read Wellmann's Greek beside it.

Greek · Wellmann

ἐοικότως δὲ καὶ τὸ βαλάνινον ἐργάζεται. δύναμιν δὲ ἔχει καθαρτικὴν σπίλων, φακῶν, ἰόνθων, οὐλῶν μελανίας, κοιλίας τε μαλακτικήν. ἔστι δὲ κακοστόμαχον· ἁρμόζει δὲ καὶ ὠταλγίαις καὶ ἤχοις καὶ συριγμοῖς μετὰ χηνείου στέατος ἐγχεόμενον.

English · approved translation

Balanos-nut oil is likewise produced in the corresponding manner. It has a capacity to clear spots, freckles, facial eruptions, and dark discoloration of scars, and to soften the bowels. It is harmful to the stomach. When instilled with goose fat, it is also suitable for earache, noises in the ears, and whistling sounds.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.34 (Wellmann 38.5–10), ed. Wellmann.

Editor's notes

Chapter 1.34 holds two entries. The Greek and English stop before ὁμοίως δὲ σκευάζεται τοῖς προειρημένοις ("Sesame oil is similarly prepared …"), ported as dioscorides-1-34-sesaminon-karyinon. Wellmann prints no section number inside the chapter; the locator gives perfume-tables' Wellmann page and lines.

The text gives no method of its own: ἐοικότως ('likewise', 'in the corresponding manner') points back to the oils just described, presumably almond oil (1.33). The nut (βάλανος) is named only in the name of the oil, so no ingredient of the preparation is listed; goose fat belongs to the uses passage.
