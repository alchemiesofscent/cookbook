ἀμαράκινον amarakinon

Marjoram unguent

Marjoram unguent family13 ingredients

Marjoram unguent is made on unripe-olive oil and balanos oil. The oils are made astringent with balsam wood, schoinos and calamus, then perfumed with marjoram, costus, amomum, nard, cassia, balsam fruit and myrrh, with honey and wine to coat the vessels and knead the chopped aromatics. Dioscorides judges the unguent made at Cyzicus the finest.

Method

  1. Make the oils astringent Make unripe-olive oil and balanos oil astringent with balsam wood, schoinos and calamus.

  2. Perfume the oils Perfume the oils with marjoram, costus, amomum, nard, cassia, balsam fruit and myrrh.

  3. Coat and knead Coat the vessels with honey and wine, and knead the chopped aromatics with them.

Other ways

A costlier unguent For a costlier unguent, add cinnamon.

What to aim for

  • Dioscorides judges the marjoram unguent made at Cyzicus the finest.
