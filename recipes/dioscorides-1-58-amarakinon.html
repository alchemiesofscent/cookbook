Marjoram unguent

draft · Marjoram unguent

Marjoram unguent, finest when made at Cyzicus. Unripe-olive oil and balanos oil are made astringent with balsam wood, schoinos and calamus, then perfumed with marjoram, costus, amomum, nard, cassia, balsam fruit and myrrh. No amounts are given.

What to aim for

Uses

Heating, stupefying, softening, opening, burning and diuretic: after surgery for putrefaction, fistulas and hydroceles; breaks open eschars and malignant ulcers; smeared round the anus for difficult urination, its inflammation and to open haemorrhoids; applied to the womb it brings on menstruation and disperses hardness and swelling; on loose felt for wounds of the muscles and sinews.

Greek · Wellmann

ἀμαράκινον δὲ ἐν Κυζίκῳ κάλλιστον γίνεται. σκευάζεται δὲ διά τε ὀμφακίνου ἐλαίου καὶ βαλανίνου, στυφομένων ξυλοβαλσάμῳ, σχοίνῳ, καλάμῳ, ἡδυνομένων δὲ ἀμαράκῳ, κόστῳ, ἀμώμῳ, νάρδῳ, κασσίᾳ, καρπῷ βαλσάμου καὶ σμύρνῃ. οἱ δὲ πολυτελέστερον αὐτὸ ποιοῦντες μίσγουσι καὶ κινάμωμον· παραλαμβάνεται δὲ μέλι καὶ οἶνος εἰς τὴν τῶν ἀγγείων χρίσιν καὶ φύραδιν τῶν κεκομμένων ἀρωμάτων. ἐστι δὲ θερμαντικόν, καρωτικόν, μαλακτικόν, ἀναστομωτικόν, 2 πυρωτικόν, οὐρητικόν, εὐχρηστοῦν εἴς τε τὰς σῆπας καὶ σύριγγας καὶ ὑδροκήλας μετὰ τὰς χειρουργίας, περιρρήττει τε τὰς ἐσχάρας καὶ τὰ τεθηριωμένα ἕλκη, πρός τε δυσουρίαν ἁρμόζει περιχριομένου τοῦ δακτυλίου, καὶ πρὸς τὰς φλεγμονὰς αὐτοῦ καὶ πρὸς αἱμορροίδων ἀναστόμωσιν περιχριόμενον, ἔμμηνά τε κινεῖ προστιθέμενον τῇ μήτρᾳ καὶ σκληρίας καὶ οἰδήματα ἐν ὑστέρᾳ διαφορεῖ, καὶ πρὸς τὰ μυότρωτα καὶ νευρότρωτα συμφέρει ἐν πιλήμασιν ἀραιοῖς βρεχόμενον καὶ ἐπιτιθέμενον.

English · approved translation

The finest marjoram unguent is made at Cyzicus. It is prepared from both omphacine oil and balanus oil, made astringent with balsam wood, schoenus, and calamus, and perfumed with marjoram, costus, amomum, nard, cassia, balsam fruit, and myrrh. Those who make it more costly also add cinnamon. Honey and wine are employed both to coat the vessels and to knead the chopped aromatics.

It has heating, stupor-inducing, softening, opening, burning, and diuretic effects. After surgical procedures, it is useful for putrefactions, fistulas, and hydroceles. It breaks open eschars and malignant ulcers. For difficulty in urination, it is suitable when smeared around the anus; it is also applied for inflammation of the anus and, when smeared around hemorrhoids, opens them. It sets menstruation in motion when applied to the womb and disperses indurations and swellings in the womb. It also benefits wounds of the muscles and sinews when pieces of loosely felted wool are soaked in it and applied.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.58.1-2, ed. Wellmann.

Editor's notes

The finest is made at Cyzicus. No amounts are given.

Those who make it more costly also add cinnamon.

Wellmann's section number 2 stands inside a sentence of the uses passage (ἀναστομωτικόν, 2 πυρωτικόν); the cut is at section 3, where the megalleion begins (dioscorides-1-58-3-megalleion; then the hedychroun report, dioscorides-1-58-3-hedychroun).

Honey and wine are moved to the working materials: the text says they are used to coat the vessels and knead the chopped aromatics, not that they are ingredients of the oil.
