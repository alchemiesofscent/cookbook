κυπρῖνον kyprinon

Henna oil

Henna oil family11 ingredientsTime · boil, cool, steep

g of ἐλαίου (Prepared oil) as written

Henna oil is made from henna flowers steeped in oil first made astringent with aspalathos, calamus, myrrh and cardamom. The oil is boiled with soaked aspalathos, then with calamus worked into myrrh and wine, strained over chopped cardamom and stirred until cool; strained again, it takes 46 lb 8 oz of henna flowers to every 28 lb and is pressed out through a basket. Dioscorides says each further steeping of fresh flowers, double or triple, makes it stronger.

Time

Boil
until the oil and aspalathos boil up together; then again with the calamus
Cool
stirring without a break, until it cools
Steep
until thoroughly steeped

No durations or temperatures are given.

Method

  1. Wash the oil Take one part of thoroughly washed unripe-olive oil and one and a half parts of rainwater.

    Pour some of the water over the oil and keep the rest for the aromatics.

    Parts A proportion: one part of oil to one and a half parts of rainwater.

  2. Boil with the aspalathos Weigh out 5½ lb (1.8 kg) of aspalathos, 6½ lb (2.1 kg) of calamus, 1 lb (322–329 g) of myrrh, 3 lb 9 oz (1.2 kg) of cardamom and 9 lb 5 oz (3–3.1 kg) of oil.

    Chop the aspalathos and soak it in water.

    Boil it with the oil until they boil up together.

  3. Add the calamus and myrrh Dissolve the myrrh in fragrant old wine and work the chopped calamus into it.

    Take out the aspalathos and add the calamus mixture to the oil.

  4. Pour over the cardamom Boil it again, then take the vessel off the heat.

    Strain the oil out of the bronze cauldron over the cardamom, chopped and worked with the rest of the rainwater.

    Stir with a spatula, without stopping, until it cools.

  5. Steep the henna flowers Strain the oil.

    Add 46 lb 8 oz (15–15.4 kg) of henna flowers to every 28 lb (9–9.2 kg) of oil and let them steep thoroughly.

    Press it out through a basket.

    28 lb of oil More oil than the 9 lb 5 oz boiled above; at the same rate, 9 lb 5 oz of oil takes about 15 lb 8 oz of flowers.

  6. A stronger oil Add the same amount of fresh flowers for a second or third steeping and press out the same way.

    Stronger Dioscorides says each further steeping makes the oil stronger.

Other ways

With cinnamon Some also mix in cinnamon.

What to aim for

  • Dioscorides advises choosing a good oil with a penetrating fragrance.
  • According to Dioscorides, each further steeping of fresh flowers makes it stronger.
