ἔλαιον ἐκ θαλλῶν ἐλαίας · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.31

Oil from young shoots

draft

An oil is made from the fatty substance of young shoots. The text gives no method; it says only which kind is best and what it is used for.

Ingredients

Amount · metric

Young shoots

λίπους τῶν θαλλῶν · θαλλός

the fatty substance (λίπος) of the young shoots

no amount given

Method

  1. 1

    Make the oil. Prepare the oil from the fatty substance of the young shoots. The text gives no method.

What to aim for

  • The best is old, thick, rich and not cloudy.

Uses

Warming: as an ointment, especially against things that obscure the pupils; rubbed on, for leprous conditions and pains around the sinews.

The text

The approved English of the passage; turn on Greek (top right) to read Wellmann's Greek beside it.

Greek · Wellmann

σκευάζεται δὲ καὶ ἐκ τοῦ λίπους τῶν θαλλῶν ἔλαιον. ἔστι δὲ αὐτοῦ κάλλιστον τὸ παλαιὸν καὶ παχὺ καὶ λιπαρόν, οὐ τεθολωμένον. θερμαίνει δὲ ἰδίως ποιοῦν πρὸς τὰ ἐπισκοτοῦντα ταῖς κόραις ἐγχριόμενον, καὶ λέπρας καὶ ἀλγήματα τὰ περὶ νεῦρα ἐγχριόμενον ὠφελεῖ.

English · approved translation

An oil is also prepared from the fatty substance of the young shoots. The best is old, thick, rich, and not cloudy. It warms and, when applied as an ointment, is especially effective against things that obscure the pupils. When rubbed on, it also benefits leprous conditions and pains around the sinews.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.31, ed. Wellmann.
