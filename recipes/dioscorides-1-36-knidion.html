διὰ τοῦ κνιδίου κόκκου · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.36 (Wellmann 39.1–5)

Cnidian-berry oil

draft

Oil of Cnidian berry is made in the same way as the oils before it, from berries that have been peeled and pounded. The text gives no amounts or further method.

Ingredients

Amount · metric

Cnidian berries

Κνιδίου κόκκου · κόκκος Κνίδιος

Peeled and pounded.

no amount given

Method

  1. 1

    Peel and pound. Peel the Cnidian berries and pound them.

  2. 2

    Make the oil. Make the oil in the same way as the oils before.

    In the same way

    The text points back to the oils just described (1.33–1.35), whose seeds or nuts are pounded, worked with hot water and pressed.

Uses

Drunk, it purges the bowels.

The text

The approved English of the passage; turn on Greek (top right) to read Wellmann's Greek beside it.

Greek · Wellmann

ὁμοίως δὲ καὶ τὸ διὰ τοῦ Κνιδίου κόκκου σκευάζεται λεπισθέντος καὶ κοπέντος. καθαρτικὴν δὲ ἔχει δύναμιν κοιλίας πινόμενον.

English · approved translation

Oil made with Cnidian berry is prepared in the same way, after the berry has been peeled and pounded. When drunk, it has a capacity to purge the bowels.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.36 (Wellmann 39.1–5), ed. Wellmann.
