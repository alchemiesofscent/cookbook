ἠδύχρουν · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.58.3

Hedychroun

draft

An unguent made on Cos. It has the same properties as marjoram unguent and is made the same way, but is more fragrant.

Ingredients

The text names no ingredients.

Method

A report, not a recipe: the text names or describes the preparation without giving a method.

What to aim for

  • More fragrant than marjoram unguent.

The text

The approved English of the passage; turn on Greek (top right) to read Wellmann's Greek beside it.

Greek · Wellmann

καὶ τὸ καλούμενον δὲ ἠδύχρουν, σκευαζόμενον δὲ ἐν Κῷ, τῆς αὐτῆς ἔχεται δυνάμεώς τε καὶ σκευασίας τῷ ἀμαρακίνῳ, εὐωδέστερον δὲ τυγχάνει.

English · approved translation

The unguent called Hedychroun, which is prepared on Cos, likewise has the same capacity and method of preparation as marjoram unguent, but is more fragrant.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.58.3, ed. Wellmann.

Editor's notes

A report, not a recipe: prepared on Cos, with the same capacity and method of preparation as marjoram unguent (dioscorides-1-58-amarakinon), but more fragrant.

The last sentence of section 3, cut from the megalleion (dioscorides-1-58-3-megalleion).

The Greek prints ἠδύχρουν with a smooth breathing; the perfume-tables lexicon has ἡδύχροον.
