μελάνθινον · Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.37 (Wellmann 39.10)

Black-seed oil

draft

A report, not a recipe: black-seed oil is prepared in the same way as radish oil and has the same capacity. The text gives no ingredients or amounts.

Ingredients

The text names no ingredients.

Method

A report, not a recipe: the text names or describes the preparation without giving a method.

Uses

The same as radish oil.

The text

The approved English of the passage; turn on Greek (top right) to read Wellmann's Greek beside it.

Greek · Wellmann

καὶ τὸ μελάνθινον δὲ τὴν αὐτὴν ἔχει δύναμιν καὶ σκευασίαν τῷ ῥαφανίνῳ.

English · approved translation

Black-seed oil has the same capacity and preparation as radish oil.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.37 (Wellmann 39.10), ed. Wellmann.

Editor's notes

A report, not a recipe: black-seed oil is said to have the same capacity and preparation as radish oil. The text names no ingredient (the black seed, μελάνθιον, only in the name of the oil).

Chapter 1.37 holds two entries; this is the last sentence of the chapter. The radish oil before it is ported as dioscorides-1-37-raphaninon. The locator gives perfume-tables' Wellmann page and line.
