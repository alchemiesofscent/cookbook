ἴρινον irinon

Iris unguent, a second way

Iris unguent family8 ingredientsTime · steep

g of ἐλαίου (Prepared oil) as written

In a second method, which Dioscorides attributes to others, iris unguent is made by steeping chopped iris in oil made astringent with balsam wood, calamus and myrrh. The oil is boiled with chopped balsam wood, which is taken out, calamus and a lump of myrrh moistened in wine are added, and 14 lb of the oil steep an equal weight of iris for two days and two nights before a hard pressing. Dioscorides says that repeating the steeping two or three times with the same weight of iris makes it more potent.

Method

  1. Boil the balsam wood Chop 5 lb 2 oz (1.7 kg) of balsam wood as finely as possible.

    Boil it with 9 lb 5 oz (3–3.1 kg) of oil.

    As finely as possible As the spathe is chopped in the first method (1.56).

  2. Add the calamus and myrrh Take out the balsam wood.

    Add 9 lb 10 oz (3.2 kg) of chopped calamus and a lump of myrrh moistened with fragrant aged wine.

  3. Steep the iris Steep the same weight of chopped iris in 14 lb (4.5–4.6 kg) of this astringed oil for two days and two nights.

  4. Press Press it out hard.

  5. A stronger unguent Steep the same weight of iris in it two or three times more, pressing it out after each steeping.

    More potent Dioscorides says each repeat makes the unguent more potent.

What to aim for

  • According to Dioscorides, each further steeping with the same weight of iris makes it more potent.
