στακτή stakte

Stacte

1 ingredient

Stacte is pressed from fresh myrrh. The myrrh is pounded with a little water and pressed through an instrument, and the oily part that runs off makes a perfume on its own. Dioscorides calls it very fragrant and costly, and rates best the stacte that is unmixed with oil and strongest in the smallest amount.

Ingredients

  • σμύρνης smyrnes

    no amount given

  • ὕδατος hydatos

Method

  1. Pound the myrrh Pound fresh myrrh with a little water.

  2. Press out the stacte Press the myrrh through an instrument and collect the oily part that runs off.

What to aim for

  • Dioscorides calls it very fragrant and costly.
  • According to Dioscorides the best is unmixed with oil and has great strength in a very small amount.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.60, ed. M. Wellmann, Berlin 1907, vol. 1, p. 55.
