κιναμώμινον kinamominon

Cinnamon unguent

8 ingredients

Cinnamon unguent has a base of balanos oil, made astringent with balsam wood, calamus and schoinos. It is perfumed with cinnamon, balsam fruit and four times as much myrrh as cinnamon, and honey goes into the kneading. Dioscorides wants it mild in scent, thick, very fragrant and bitter, its thickness coming from myrrh, not resin.

Method

  1. Prepare the oil Make balanos oil astringent with balsam wood, calamus and schoinos.

  2. Add the aromatics Add cinnamon, balsam fruit and four times as much myrrh as cinnamon.

  3. Knead with honey Mix honey in during the kneading.

What to aim for

  • Dioscorides approves one that is not acrid but mild in scent, with a hint of myrrh, thick, very fragrant and bitter to the taste.
  • According to Dioscorides such thickness comes from myrrh, not resin, since resin gives neither bitterness nor fragrance.
