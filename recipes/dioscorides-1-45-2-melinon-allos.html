μηλῖνον melinon

Quince oil, a second way

Quince oil family2 ingredientsTime · wrap, soak

In this second quince oil the fruit goes into the oil itself. The quinces are wrapped in cloth for ten days to hold in their fragrance, then soaked in the oil for two days and nights and pressed out. Dioscorides gives no quantities.

Time

Wrap
ten days
Soak
two days and nights

No temperatures or amounts are given.

Ingredients

  • μῆλα mela

    Wrapped in cloth for ten days first.

    no amount given

  • ἐλαίῳ elaio

    Perhaps the prepared oil of 1.45.1, or plain olive oil.

    no amount given

Method

  1. Wrap the quinces Wrap the quinces in cloth for ten days, so that the fragrance is held in and does not escape.

  2. Soak in the oil Soak the fruit in the oil for two days and nights.

  3. Press and store Press it out and put the oil away.

What to aim for

  • Dioscorides approves the oil that smells of quinces.

Source

Dioscorides, De materia medica 1.45.2, ed. M. Wellmann, Berlin 1907, vol. 1, p. 44.
