νάρδινον nardinon

Nard unguent

Nard unguent family14 ingredients

Nard unguent is made on balanos oil or unripe-olive oil, with or without malabathron leaf. The oil is made astringent with schoinos and perfumed with costus, amomum, nard, myrrh and balsam. A plain version uses unripe-olive oil, schoinos, calamus, costus and nard.

Method

  1. Choose the oil Take balanos oil or unripe-olive oil, with or without malabathron leaf.

  2. Make the oil astringent Add schoinos to make the oil astringent.

  3. Add the aromatics Add costus, amomum, nard, myrrh and balsam for fragrance.

Other ways

A plain version For a plain version, use unripe-olive oil, schoinos, calamus, costus and nard.

What to aim for

  • Dioscorides approves one that is thin and not acrid, with the fragrance of dried nard or amomum.
  • According to Dioscorides it is fluid, not sticky, unless it contains resin.
