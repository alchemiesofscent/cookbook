What scholars identify it as

Identifications are gathered by headword; a scholar’s entry may concern another sense of the word.

  • Amigues 2006: Iris germanica L. var. florentina (page 291)
  • Amigues 2006: Iris pallida Lam. (page 291)
  • Beck 2020: Iris florentina L. (row 36; Dsc. I, 1)
  • Beck 2020: Iris germanica L. (row 36; Dsc. I, 1)
  • Beck 2020: Iris pallida Lamk. (row 36; Dsc. I, 1)
  • Hort 1916: Iris pallida etc. (page 490) as ἴρις
  • Keyser and Irby-Massie 2008: some Iris species, often Iris pseudacorus L. or Iris pallida Lam. (page 1045)
