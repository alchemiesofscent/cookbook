What scholars identify it as

Identifications are gathered by headword; a scholar’s entry may concern another sense of the word.

  • Amigues 2006: Calamintha nepeta (L.) Savi. (p. 333)
  • Beck 2020: Nasturtium officinale R. Br. (row 24; Dsc. II, 128)
  • Haars 2018: Clinopodium nepeta subsp. glandulosum (Req.) Govaerts (p. 373; Orib. 1,18,33; Kühn 12,124 K.) as σισύμβριον
  • Haars 2018: Mentha arvensis L. (p. 373; Orib. 1,18,33; Kühn 12,124 K.) as σισύμβριον
  • Haars 2018: Nasturtium officinale R. Br. (p. 373; Orib. 1,18,34; Kühn 12,124 K.) as σισύμβριον
  • Hort 1916: Mentha aquatica (p. 490)
