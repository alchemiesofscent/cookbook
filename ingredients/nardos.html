English words for it

These pages say “nard” (Alchemies of Scent translation, Dioscorides 1.62, 1.58.1–2, 2.76.8–10). LSJ s.v. νάρδος: “spikenard, Nardostachys Jatamansi”. Tap the word on a recipe to use another.
