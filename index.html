Flags for review (55)
  • 1.25.1-2 Kyphi ἀρκευθίδος: equal amount: no unit; the same as the cyperus, i.e. half a xestes
  • 1.30.1-2 Unripe-olive oil perfume-tables gives the locator as 1.30, overlapping the three later recipes of the chapter; cut to 1.30.1-2. The only procedure in it is the imitation of aged oil, not a preparation of omphakinon.
  • 1.30.3-4 Whitened oil δαλὸν πιτυίνης: equal weight: no unit; presumably equal to the fifty drachmai of fenugreek
  • 1.30.3-4 Whitened oil ἴρεως: equal amount: no unit; equal to the melilot, eleven holkai
  • 1.30.3-4 Whitened oil οἴνῳ παλαιῷ: not in perfume-tables; a rinse for the vessel, not mixed in as such
  • 1.30.3 Wild-olive oil no method in the text: the single step only says the oil is made from the wild olive. Locator 1.30.3 covers the first part of the section only; the whitened oil begins in the same section.
  • 1.31 Olive honey no preparation in the text (a natural exudation), so no steps; the only item, water, belongs to the dose. Perhaps a report rather than a recipe.
  • 1.32.1 Castor oil κροτώνων: as much as seems fit: no amount
  • 1.39 Myrtle oil ἐλαίου ὀμφακίνου: equal amount: no unit; as much oil as the juice pressed from the leaves
  • 1.43 Rose oil ἐλαίου: the text does not say whether this is a further batch of oil made astringent in the same way
  • 1.44 Palm-spathe oil ὀμφάκινον ἔλαιον: equal weight: no unit; the oil and the palm spathe are to be equal in weight
  • 1.45.2 Quince oil, a second way ἐλαίῳ: τῷ ἐλαίῳ, "the oil": perhaps the oil prepared as in 1.45.1, or plain olive oil; the text does not say
  • 1.48 Marjoram oil ἐλαίου ὀμφακίνου: not in perfume-tables; relative amount: no unit
  • 1.49 Basil oil ἐλαίου: not in perfume-tables; equal amount: no unit; presumably the same 20 litrai as the first charge
  • 1.49 Basil oil ὠκίμου: equal amount: no unit; presumably the same 11 litrai 8 ounces as the first charge
  • 1.49 Basil oil ὀμφοκίνου: not in perfume-tables; the Greek prints ὀμφοκίνου, presumably for ὀμφακίνου
  • 1.52.1-4 Lily oil ἅλας: not in perfume-tables
  • 1.52.1-4 Lily oil ἐλαίου: not in perfume-tables; equal amount: no unit; the same as the first charge, presumably 3½ litrai
  • 1.52.1-4 Lily oil κιναμώμου: perfume-tables reads καρδαμώμου here; the oribasius-app Greek and the approved English have cinnamon
  • 1.52.1-4 Lily oil κρόκου: equal amount: saffron equal to the cinnamon; no unit
  • 1.52.1-4 Lily oil κιναμώμου: equal amount: cinnamon equal to the saffron; no unit
  • 1.52.1-4 Lily oil ὕδατι ὀμερίῳ: the Greek prints ὀμερίῳ; presumably ὀμβρίῳ (rainwater, as the English has): check Wellmann
  • 1.52.1-4 Lily oil μέλιτι: not in perfume-tables; a coating for hands and vessels rather than a charge
  • 1.53 Narcissus unguent σμύρνης: a lump (χόνδρον): no unit
  • 1.53 Narcissus unguent ἐλαίου: not in perfume-tables; the amount is lost in the lacuna
  • 1.53 Narcissus unguent ναρκίσσου: as much as possible: no unit
  • 1.54 Saffron unguent ἐλαίου: equal amount: no unit; presumably the same 3½ litrai as the first charge
  • 1.55 Henna oil ἐλαίου ὀμφακίνου: relative amount (one part to one and a half parts of rainwater): no unit
  • 1.55 Henna oil ἐλαίου: not in perfume-tables; the oil made above is only 9 litrai 5 ounces, so the 28 litrai is a ratio or a larger batch: the text does not say
  • 1.55 Henna oil ἄνθους: equal amount: no unit; the same as the first charge, presumably 46 litrai 8 ounces
  • 1.55 Henna oil ὕδατος ὀμβρίου: relative amount (one and a half parts to one part of oil): no unit
  • 1.56.1,3-4 Iris unguent μέλιτι: not in perfume-tables; a coating for the vessel rather than a charge
  • 1.56.1,3-4 Iris unguent Cut: sections 1 and 3-4 joined with […] in Greek and English; section 2 is the separate recipe dioscorides-1-56-2-irinon-allos. Check the cut.
  • 1.56.2 Iris unguent, a second way καλτάμου: the oribasius-app Greek prints καλτάμου, a slip for καλάμου (perfume-tables reads καλάμου; the English has "aromatic reed"); copied as it stands
  • 1.56.2 Iris unguent, a second way σμύρνης: a lump: no unit
  • 1.56.2 Iris unguent, a second way ἴρεως: equal amount: the same weight as the 14 litrai of oil; no unit
  • 1.56.2 Iris unguent, a second way ἴρεως: not in perfume-tables; equal amount: no unit; the same weight as before
  • 1.58.3 Megalleion τερεβινθίνη: the resin of the item above, specified (said of perfumes in general), not a second charge
  • 1.58.3 Megalleion Cut: section 3 split at the sentence boundary before καὶ τὸ καλούμενον δὲ ἠδύχρουν; the hedychroun is a separate report. Check the cut.
  • 1.61 Cinnamon unguent σμύρνης: four times as much as the cinnamon, which has no amount: no unit. The Greek prints παράὰ (sic), presumably for παρὰ
  • 1.62 Nard unguent σχοίνου: not in perfume-tables
  • 1.62 Nard unguent κόστου: not in perfume-tables
  • 1.62 Nard unguent νάρδου: not in perfume-tables
  • 1.62 Nard unguent one perfume-tables record covers both the full unguent and the plain version (λιτή) of the last sentence; the plain version's ingredients are listed with role 'alternative'. Check whether it should be a recipe of its own.
  • 1.63.apparatus Iasmelaion ἰοῦ: the Greek accents ἰοῦ, the genitive of ἰός (verdigris), not ἴου of ἴον (violet); taken as the violet, as perfume-tables' context and the flowers require
  • 1.63.apparatus Iasmelaion σησαμίνου ἐλαίου: not in perfume-tables
  • 1.63.apparatus Iasmelaion No approved English: the English is a draft translation from the Greek (round 3 item 10), and the Greek comes from perfume-tables, which takes it from Wellmann's apparatus to 1.63, not from the oribasius-app text.
  • 1.63 Malabathron unguent σμύρνης: more than in nard unguent (1.62), which gives no amount: no unit
  • 1.71.5 Resin processed with water ὀμερίου ὕδατος: the Greek prints ὀμερίου; presumably ὀμβρίου (rainwater, as the English has): check Wellmann
  • 1.71.6 Resin processed without water ῥητίνης: section 6 does not name the resin it burns; the only form of ῥητίνη in the cut is in λιγνὺς ῥητίνης (resin soot), used here as the form
  • 1.71.6 Resin processed without water ξηρὰς: the form is the adjective alone; the noun ῥητίνας is understood
  • 7.20.4 Rose oil Ῥόδων: perfume-tables gives the amount as 'α 𐆄 γ'; the α is read with νυχθήμερον ('one day-and-night', as the approved English and Aët. 1.113 ἡμέραν καὶ νύκτα 𐆄 γ), so the amount is 𐆄 γ, three ounces
  • 7.20.4 Rose oil ἐλαίου ὀμφακίνου: Italian sextarius taken to be the xestes of units.json (0.547 l)
  • 1.113 Rose oil ἐλαίου ὀμφακίνου: Italian sextarius taken to be the xestes of units.json (0.547 l)
  • 1.113 Rose oil Cut: the text joins two separate stretches of the chapter, marked […]; the chapter heading (which names the wax-salve too) is left out and the closing passage on drinking the finest rose oil is kept with the oil. Check the cut.
